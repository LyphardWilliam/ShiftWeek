ShiftWeek

Plan shifts, tap in, check pay.

For students juggling casual jobs. Put every employer's shifts in one week, clock in with one tap, and check each payslip against your own records.

Coming soon to the App Store. Free, no ads, no account.

What it does

  • Week – all your shifts from every job, Monday to Sunday, with paid hours and estimated pay.
  • Today – tap a shift card to clock in, tap again to clock out.
  • Pay – enter the hours and gross pay from a payslip and get Match, Close or Check with employer.
  • Student visa mode – optional: see your busiest fortnight, with a reminder from 40 hours.
  • Widget – this week's hours, estimated pay and your next shift on the Home Screen.

Support

Is the pay amount exact?

No. ShiftWeek estimates pay from the hourly rate and multipliers you enter. It does not look up award rates. For official figures, use the Fair Work Pay Calculator.

Why can't I clock in?

You can clock in only on the day the shift starts, and only one shift at a time. Clock out of your other shift first.

I tapped a card by mistake.

Long-press the card and choose Clear Clock Times. Your rostered times are used again.

The widget says "Open ShiftWeek".

Open the app once. If it keeps happening, check Settings → About → Home Screen widget.

Student visa limits

Work limits can change. Always check the Department of Home Affairs for the current rules.
