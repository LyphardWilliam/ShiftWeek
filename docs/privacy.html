Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version

ShiftWeek does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your jobs, shifts, clock times and payslip figures are saved in a database on your device. The ShiftWeek Home Screen widget reads the same database on the same device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no server and no sync.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.

Links

The app links to the Fair Work Pay Calculator. It opens in Safari only when you tap it. That website has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete a job in Settings to remove its shifts and payslips, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

ShiftWeek is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
